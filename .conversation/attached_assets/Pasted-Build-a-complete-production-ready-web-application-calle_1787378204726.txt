# 1. PURPOSE OF THE APPLICATION

The application should help a user analyze stocks for **short-term swing trading**, with a typical holding period of approximately **a few days to several weeks**.

The application should answer questions such as:

* Is this stock currently attractive for a swing trade?
* Should I BUY, HOLD, or AVOID/SELL?
* What is the expected short-term price direction?
* What is the confidence level of the prediction?
* What are the important technical factors behind the signal?
* What could be a reasonable entry zone?
* What could be a reasonable stop-loss?
* What could be reasonable profit targets?
* What is the risk/reward ratio?
* What could invalidate the trade?
* What is the overall strength of the setup?

The application is a **decision-support and educational tool**, not a guarantee of future stock prices or investment returns.
